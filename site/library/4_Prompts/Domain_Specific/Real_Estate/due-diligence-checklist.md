# Property Due Diligence Checklist

## Purpose
Build a diligence checklist ordered by what could kill the deal, so the expensive discoveries happen before the money is hard.

## Instructions
Act as an acquisitions manager. Produce a diligence checklist for the transaction below. Order items by the cost of finding out late — a survey issue discovered after the deposit goes hard is a different problem from the same issue found in week one.

Inputs:
- **Property:** [type, size, location, age, current use]
- **Transaction:** [purchase price, diligence period length, deposit structure and when it goes hard]
- **Financing:** [lender requirements, if any]
- **Known issues:** [anything already disclosed]
- **Intended use:** [same as current, or a change of use]

Cover:
1. **Title and survey** — commitment, exceptions read individually, easements, encroachments, access, restrictive covenants
2. **Zoning and entitlement** — current classification, conformity, whether the intended use is permitted as of right, open permits, certificates of occupancy
3. **Physical** — property condition assessment, roof, structure, HVAC, electrical, plumbing, remaining useful life per system, with a cost and a year attached
4. **Environmental** — Phase I, and the conditions under which a Phase II follows
5. **Financial** — rent roll tied to leases, T-12 tied to bank statements, tax reassessment on sale, insurance quoted fresh, utility history
6. **Leases and tenants** — abstracts, estoppel certificates, subordination agreements, security deposit accounting
7. **Legal** — litigation, liens, code violations, association obligations
8. **Operational** — service contracts and their assignability or termination, staffing, warranties

For each item give: who performs it, the lead time, the cost, the deal-breaker threshold, and the date it must complete by, counted back from the deposit going hard.

## Output Format
- Checklist table: item, owner, lead time, cost, deal-breaker threshold, due date
- A critical path showing which items must start immediately
- A go/no-go decision framework for the end of the diligence period
