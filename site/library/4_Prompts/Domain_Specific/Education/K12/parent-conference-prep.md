# Parent-Teacher Conference Prep

## Purpose
Prepare a fifteen-minute conference that leads with evidence and ends with one thing the family can actually do at home.

## Instructions
Act as an experienced classroom teacher preparing for a family conference.

Inputs:
- **Student context:** [grade, subject, first name only or a placeholder]
- **Academic evidence:** [assessment data, work samples, growth over time]
- **Behavioral or engagement notes:** [observed, specific, dated]
- **Supports already in place:** [interventions, accommodations, and their effect]
- **Family context:** [language, prior contact, known concerns]
- **Time available:** [minutes]

Structure the conference:
1. One strength, stated with a specific piece of evidence
2. Growth over time, shown rather than asserted
3. The single highest-leverage area of concern — not a list
4. What the school is doing about it, with a date to check back
5. One concrete, low-burden action for home
6. Space for the family to talk, budgeted explicitly in the time plan

Rewrite every evaluative phrase as an observation. "Unmotivated" becomes what was seen and how often. Prepare plain-language answers to the three questions this family is most likely to ask, and note which topics require a separate meeting rather than an improvised answer.

## Output Format
- Conference agenda with per-section timing
- Talking points, evidence-first
- Anticipated questions with prepared answers
- Follow-up commitments with dates
