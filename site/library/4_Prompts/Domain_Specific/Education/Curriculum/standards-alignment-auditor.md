# Standards Alignment Auditor

## Purpose
Audit existing materials against the standards they claim to cover, checking depth of knowledge and not just topic overlap.

## Instructions
Act as a curriculum auditor. Review the materials below against the standards they are labeled with.

Inputs:
- **Standards set:** [Common Core, NGSS, state, or program-specific — name it]
- **Standards claimed:** [list, by code]
- **Materials:** [paste lessons, tasks, or assessments]
- **Grade and subject:** [text]

For each claimed standard report:
- **Coverage:** fully addressed, partially addressed, or mentioned only
- **Depth match:** the cognitive demand of the standard's verb against the cognitive demand of the task, with both named
- **Evidence:** the specific activity or item that addresses it
- **Time:** roughly how much instructional time it receives

Topic overlap is the trap. A standard that says "construct an argument from evidence" is not covered by a worksheet naming the same content — say so explicitly when it happens.

Then report across the set: standards claimed but not covered, standards covered but not claimed, standards over-served relative to their weight, and any standard where the assessment is easier than the instruction.

## Output Format
- Per-standard audit table
- Cross-set findings in the four categories above
- Prioritized fixes, highest-leverage first
