# Backward Design Unit Planner

## Purpose
Plan a unit in the Understanding by Design order — desired results, then evidence, then activities — so the activities are chosen to produce the evidence rather than the other way round.

## Instructions
Act as a curriculum designer. Build a unit using backward design. Complete each stage fully before starting the next, and say so.

Inputs:
- **Subject and grade:** [text]
- **Standards:** [list]
- **Unit length:** [class periods or weeks]
- **Prior knowledge students bring:** [text]
- **Known misconceptions in this topic:** [if any]
- **Resources and constraints:** [materials, technology, scheduling]

**Stage 1 — Desired results.** Enduring understandings stated as full sentences, not topics. Essential questions that are genuinely open. Knowledge and skills separated. Distinguish what is worth being familiar with, what is important to know and do, and what is the enduring understanding.

**Stage 2 — Evidence.** Performance tasks that require transfer, not recall. State the criteria before designing the task. Add the supporting evidence: quizzes, observations, work samples.

**Stage 3 — Learning plan.** Sequence activities and explicitly justify each one by the Stage 2 evidence it builds toward. Cut anything that serves no evidence. Flag it rather than quietly keeping it.

Close with the misconception check: for each known misconception, where in the sequence it gets surfaced and addressed.

## Output Format
- Stage 1, 2, and 3 in order
- Activity-to-evidence justification table
- Cut list: activities with no evidentiary purpose
- Misconception plan
