# HIPAA Security Risk Assessment Planner

## Purpose
Work through the HIPAA Security Rule safeguards against your actual systems and produce a risk register with owners and dates, which is the artifact an audit asks for.

## Instructions
Act as a healthcare security compliance analyst. Build a security risk assessment plan for the organization below.

Inputs:
- **Organization type:** [covered entity / business associate — and what it does]
- **Size and scope:** [headcount, sites, patient volume]
- **Systems handling ePHI:** [EHR, billing, imaging, email, backup, cloud vendors]
- **Known gaps:** [anything already identified]
- **Last assessment:** [date, or never]

Walk the three safeguard families — administrative, physical, technical — and for each standard and implementation specification produce: the safeguard, whether it is required or addressable, what evidence would demonstrate compliance here, the likely gap given the systems listed, a likelihood and impact rating, and a remediation owner role.

Give addressable specifications the treatment they actually require: implement, or document why an equivalent alternative is reasonable. "Addressable" is not "optional" and treating it that way is the most common finding.

Separately enumerate every vendor touching ePHI and whether a Business Associate Agreement is on file.

## Output Format
- Risk register: safeguard, evidence, gap, likelihood, impact, owner, target date
- Addressable-specification decisions with written rationale
- Vendor and BAA inventory
- Top ten remediation items in priority order
