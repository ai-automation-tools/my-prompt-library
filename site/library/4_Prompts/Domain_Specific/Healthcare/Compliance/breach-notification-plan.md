# Breach Notification Response Plan

## Purpose
Build the decision tree and notification artifacts for a suspected PHI breach before one happens, since the clock starts at discovery and not at confirmation.

## Instructions
Act as a healthcare privacy officer. Produce a breach response plan and the notification templates it will need.

Inputs:
- **Organization:** [covered entity / business associate, size, states of operation]
- **Systems in scope:** [where ePHI lives]
- **Existing incident response process:** [paste, or say "none"]
- **Cyber insurance carrier notification terms:** [if any]

Produce:
1. **Risk assessment decision tree.** Walk the four-factor assessment — nature and extent of the PHI including identifiers and likelihood of re-identification; who the unauthorized recipient was; whether the PHI was actually acquired or viewed; and the extent to which risk has been mitigated. Show what evidence answers each factor and who gathers it.
2. **Exception check** for the low-probability-of-compromise determination and the three regulatory exceptions, with the documentation each requires.
3. **Notification matrix.** Individuals, HHS, media, business associates upstream, and state regulators — each with trigger, deadline, required content, and method. Note where state law is stricter than HIPAA and therefore controls.
4. **Templates.** Individual notice, substitute notice, HHS submission summary, media statement.
5. **Timeline** counting from discovery, not from confirmation, with the named owner for each step.

Call out explicitly: the sixty-day outer limit for individual notice, the five-hundred-individual threshold that changes media and HHS timing, and the annual reporting path for smaller breaches.

## Output Format
- Decision tree
- Notification matrix
- Notice templates
- Timeline with owners
