# Review Request Campaign Designer

## Purpose
Design a review request flow timed to when the customer has actually formed an opinion, and keep it inside the platform and advertising rules that govern incentives.

## Instructions
Act as a lifecycle marketing specialist.

Inputs:
- **Product and use cycle:** [how long before a customer can judge it]
- **Delivery data available:** [tracking, delivery confirmation]
- **Current review rate and volume:** [text]
- **Channels:** [email, SMS, in-package insert, post-purchase page]
- **Platform:** [own store, Amazon, Google — their incentive rules differ]

Design the flow:
- **Timing** anchored to delivery plus the time to form an opinion, not to purchase. A mattress and a phone case are not the same wait.
- **Ask structure:** one specific question, not "leave a review". A prompt that asks what problem the product solved produces a usable review; an open box produces a rating and no text.
- **Photo and video requests** made separately and later, since asking for both at once lowers completion of each.
- **Negative experience path** that routes to support before the public form — this must be a genuine service path, not a filter. Suppressing negative reviews while soliciting positive ones is review gating and is prohibited on most platforms and actionable under advertising rules.
- **Incentives**, if used, offered to all reviewers regardless of sentiment, disclosed, and checked against each platform's rules, which vary and are stricter than general advertising law in places.

Include a measurement plan: review rate by touchpoint, text length, photo attachment rate, and the effect on conversion of the products that gained reviews.

## Output Format
- Flow with timing, channel, and copy per touchpoint
- Negative experience routing
- Incentive and disclosure rules per platform
- Measurement plan
