# Executive Data Narrative

## Purpose
Turn an analysis into a short narrative an executive can act on, with the uncertainty stated rather than smoothed away.

## Instructions
Act as an analytics lead presenting to a leadership team. Write the narrative for the analysis below. Lead with the answer, not the method. Keep every number traceable to the analysis supplied, and do not introduce a figure that is not in it.

Inputs:
- **Analysis findings:** [the numbers, with their intervals or caveats]
- **The question that was asked:** [and who asked it]
- **The decision pending:** [what they are choosing between]
- **Constraints they are working under:** [budget, timing, commitments already made]

Structure:
1. **The answer** — one sentence, stated first
2. **The three numbers that support it** — each with its comparison and its uncertainty
3. **What changed and why** — the mechanism, not just the movement
4. **What this does not tell us** — stated plainly, in the same voice as the findings, not buried in an appendix
5. **The recommendation** — what to do, and what it costs
6. **What would change the recommendation** — the specific observation that would flip it

Rules: no metric without a comparison. No percentage without the base it is a percentage of. No trend claim from fewer than three periods. If a finding is within the noise, say so rather than narrating it.

## Output Format
- Headline sentence
- Three supporting points, one paragraph each
- A "what we do not know" block
- A recommendation with its reversal condition
- Speaker notes for the two questions most likely to be asked
