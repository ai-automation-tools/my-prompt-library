# Zero-Based Budget Rebuild

## Purpose
Rebuild a department or household budget from zero instead of last year's number plus a percentage, so every line has to justify itself against a purpose.

## Instructions
Act as a finance business partner running a zero-based review. Do not anchor on the prior budget: build each line from the activity that drives it, then compare to the prior figure at the end. Lines that exist only because they existed last year are the point of the exercise, so flag them rather than smoothing them.

Inputs:
- **Entity:** [department, team, project or household]
- **Period:** [fiscal year or months covered]
- **Prior-period actuals:** [line items with amounts; paste a table]
- **Mandate:** [what this entity must deliver, in two or three sentences]
- **Hard constraints:** [contracts, headcount commitments, legal or tax obligations that cannot move]
- **Target:** [total ceiling, or required saving as a number or percentage]

Steps:
1. **Classify every prior line** as committed (cannot change this period), driven (cost follows a volume you can name) or discretionary (no driver beyond habit). Show the totals for each class.
2. **Rebuild driven lines bottom-up:** unit cost x expected volume, with the volume source stated. If you have to assume a volume, mark it as an assumption.
3. **Put discretionary lines through a justification test:** what outcome does this buy, what happens if it is cut by half, and what is the cheapest way to get the same outcome?
4. **Rank the discretionary lines** by value per unit of spend and draw the cut line where the total meets the target.
5. **Reconcile to the prior budget:** for every line that moved more than 10%, give one sentence of reasoning.
6. **List the risks of the cuts:** what breaks first, who will notice, and which cut is cheapest to reverse mid-period.

## Output Format
- Table: line, class, prior actual, rebuilt amount, driver or justification, change %
- The ranked discretionary list with the cut line marked
- A short "what we are not funding" list so the trade-offs are visible
- Assumptions register: every number that rests on a guess, with who can confirm it
