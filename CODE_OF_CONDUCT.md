# Code of Conduct

This is a small project with one maintainer. The rules are short.

**Be decent.** Treat everyone in issues, pull requests, and discussions with respect.
Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, slurs, personal attacks, or unwelcome
sexual attention, in any channel connected to this project.

**Keep it on topic.** Prompt content, the app, and the tooling around them. Political and
religious argument, advertising, and recruiting belong elsewhere.

**Respect other people's work.** Attribute what you vendor. Do not submit content you do
not have the right to publish, and do not add other people's personal information
(names paired with emails, phone numbers, addresses) to the library.

**Respect the content policy.** Prompts that exist to produce harassment, hate, malware,
or sexual content involving minors will be removed and the submitter blocked.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, remove, or reject any contribution and may block anyone who
violates this document. Report problems through a private GitHub advisory or the support
email in the README. Reports are handled in confidence.

Adapted loosely from the Contributor Covenant 2.1, cut down to what a single-maintainer
repository needs.
